Nook 0.3.9

- **What's new card**: after every update Nook now shows what changed, right under the notch.
- **Brightness**: a brightness popup when you change it (laptops), and a Brightness option for the island circle.
- **Lyrics retry**: if a song's lyrics aren't found the first time, Nook tries again a few times before giving up.
- **Smoother animation** and lighter drawing.
- **New Settings layout** with a sidebar of icons, sections and a banner.
